### Step 2: Get Licensed (1-3 weeks)
**Exact Steps:**

#### 2.1 Pre-Licensing Course (20 hours)
- [ ] Go to: https://www.lytespeed.net/ or https://www.insuranceexam.org/
- [ ] Pick California Life Insurance 20-hour course (~$100-150)
- [ ] Schedule: Start Monday, complete by Friday
- [ ] Time commitment: 2-3 hours/day
- [ ] Cost: $120

#### 2.2 Take Licensing Exam
- [ ] Register: California Department of Insurance (https://www.insurance.ca.gov/)
- [ ] Exam fee: $240
- [ ] Schedule: Prometric testing center (anywhere, same day results)
- [ ] Pass rate: ~70% on first try with course
- [ ] You'll pass.

#### 2.3 Background Check & License Approval
- [ ] Submit: Background check (clean background = auto-approve)
- [ ] Cost: $50-75
- [ ] Wait: 5-7 business days
- [ ] Arrive: Physical license in mail

**Total for licensing:**
- Time: 2-3 weeks
- Cost: ~$410-465
- Outcome: Active Life Insurance Agent License

## PHASE 4: FIRST SALES

### Step 7: Your First 5 Clients (Week 4-6)
**Exact workflow:**

#### 7.1 Initial Call (15 min)
- [ ] Ask: "Age, health, income, dependents, current coverage?"
- [ ] Assess: How much they need (typically 10x income)
- [ ] Present: 1-3 options (term life, whole life)
- [ ] Close: "I'll send you applications"

#### 7.2 Application (takes them 10 min)
- [ ] Send: Digital app + docs (via DocuSign)
- [ ] They: Sign + submit
- [ ] You: Submit to carrier

#### 7.3 Underwriting (3-7 days)
- [ ] Carrier: Reviews app, may order medical exam
- [ ] Exam: Takes 30 min, they book it
- [ ] Approval: Comes back, policy issued

#### 7.4 Commission Payment
- [ ] Receive: 60-110% first-year commission
- [ ] Example: $500/month term life = $50-55 first month commission
- [ ] Recurring: 10% of their premium for 2-5 years

**Your first 5 clients at $300-500/mo average:**
- First month: $1,500-2,750 in commissions
- Month 2-3: $150-275/mo recurring (while you sell more)
- Month 6: 20 clients × $400 = $8,000/mo in commissions + recurring
